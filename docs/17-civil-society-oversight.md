# 17 · Civil Society Oversight — Participation Mechanisms

## Formal consultation on intelligence legislation

- Draft intelligence legislation is published for 30 days of public consultation before first reading
- CSO submissions are published alongside the draft; the JPC must respond in writing to each
  substantive submission
- IG regulatory proposals follow the same rule

## Access to unclassified oversight reports

- JPC and IG annual reports are published in unclassified versions within 30 days of adoption
- Redactions are limited to sources, methods and ongoing operations; each redaction carries a
  logged reason code
- Over-redaction is appealable to the JPC

## Protection for CSO researchers

- Statutory prohibition on collecting information about lawful advocacy and research activity
- Anti-SLAPP protections per EU Directive 2024/1069: early dismissal and cost-shifting
- A dedicated IG contact point receives complaints from researchers about suspected surveillance or
  obstruction
- The Ombudsman can trigger an IG review on a CSO complaint (existing flow T17)

## Annual public forum

- One open JPC session per year with CSOs, academia and media
- The IG presents the unclassified annual report and answers questions
- Minutes and video are published; follow-up questions are answered in writing within 30 days

## The Danish model: agonistic accountability

The Danish CSO Accountability Study (2026) describes accountability not as periodic reporting but as
**agonistic accountability** — a standing, institutionalized contest between the service, its
overseers and the public. Key elements adopted here:

- Oversight works when overseer and service maintain an adversarial posture, not a collegial one
- Civil society supplies the friction that keeps review credible: questions, challenges,
  counter-expertise
- The goal is not harmony but predictable, rule-bound disagreement that produces better findings

Applied to this repo: the Public layer (CSO, media, academia) is not a passive audience of the JPC
report — it is a party to the oversight loop (`diagrams/oversight-layering.mmd`).
