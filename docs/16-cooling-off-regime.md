# 16 · Cooling-Off Regime — Post-Employment Restrictions

> DEMO // UNCLASSIFIED — SIMULATED DATA — NO REAL SYSTEMS

## Purpose

Prevents the conversion of state secrets, personal networks and procurement knowledge into private
profit, and blocks the revolving door into foreign or hostile employers.

## Tiered cooling-off periods

| Tier | Positions | Cooling-off period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Directors, Deputy Directors, heads of counterintelligence/SIGINT | 24 months |
| T2 | Department heads, senior analysts, procurement officers | 12 months |
| T3 | Operational staff with access to classified sources/methods | 6 months |

## Prohibited destinations

- Work for a foreign intelligence or security service: **lifetime ban, no exceptions**
- Lobbying or consulting for foreign governments or state-owned companies: lifetime ban for T1;
  24 months for T2/T3
- Companies in whose procurement or licensing decisions the official participated: 24 months after
  the last such decision
- Roles requiring use of still-classified knowledge (e.g., training a competitor's analysts on
  sources and methods): prohibited while the knowledge remains classified

## Exception approval process

- Written application filed at least 30 days before the new employment starts
- T1 exceptions require IG recommendation plus JPC approval; T2/T3 are decided by the IG alone
- All exceptions are published in a public register with reasons

## Enforcement mechanisms

- Pre-departure declaration filed at least 30 days before exit
  (`schemas/post-employment-notification.schema.json`)
- IG employment database: every departing official is registered for the duration of the cooling-off
  period
- Cross-referencing with procurement records to detect ex-officials advising winning bidders
- Pension and payroll records checked during the cooling-off period

## Consequences of violation

- Pension reduction or suspension for the duration of the violation
- Recovery of remuneration received from the prohibited employer
- Criminal referral for false declaration
- The prohibited employer may be barred from public contracts for up to 5 years

## Annual public reporting

The IG publishes an aggregated annual report (`schemas/cooling-off-compliance-report.schema.json`)
covering departures, destinations, exceptions, investigations and penalties. It is laid before the
JPC and published publicly. The enforcement flow is shown in `diagrams/cooling-off-enforcement.mmd`.

## Comparative anchors

- **U.S. DOJ — ODNI post-employment violation case (Deirdre Walsh, 2026):** a senior ODNI official
  moved to a company linked to a foreign government without the mandatory post-employment review.
  Illustrates that declarations without enforcement are inert — the enforcement action itself is the
  deterrent.
- **Rep. Jackson — FY2027 Intelligence Authorization Act amendments (2026):** statutory extension of
  cooling-off for additional categories and civil monetary penalties with IG claw-back authority.
  Illustrates that cooling-off regimes must be tightened by statute as evasion patterns emerge.
