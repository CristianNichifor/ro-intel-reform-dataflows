# 14 · Leadership Appointment — Depoliticized Selection & Confirmation

> DEMO // UNCLASSIFIED — SIMULATED DATA — NO REAL SYSTEMS

## Purpose

Defines a depoliticized, parliamentary-controlled process for appointing the Directors and Deputy
Directors of SRI and SIE. The goal: no director who owes the position to a single political patron,
and no removal without a public, reasoned procedure.

## Independent Selection Committee

- **Composition** — 7 members, appointed on staggered 5-year terms:
  - 2 nominated by the JPC (one per chamber)
  - 1 retired judge, proposed by the Superior Council of Magistracy (CSM)
  - 1 academic in security studies or constitutional law, proposed by the Romanian Academy
  - 1 former senior public servant with clearance-vetting experience, proposed by ANI
  - 2 civil-society/academia members proposed by the Legal Committees of the two chambers
- **Incompatibilities** — members may not belong to a political party, may not have worked for an
  intelligence service in the last 5 years, and may not apply for the position they are screening.
- **Work product** — confidential interviews, background vetting orders, and a written report with a
  shortlist of 3–5 candidates; the report is published with security redactions.

## Objective qualification criteria

- Romanian citizenship only; no dual citizenship
- Minimum 15 years of professional experience, of which at least 5 in national security, public
  administration, or justice
- No political-party membership or elected office in the last 5 years (North Macedonia model)
- Active TS/SCI clearance
- Clean ANI asset-declaration verification; no conflicts of interest (see doc 15)
- No criminal record and no disciplinary findings by a judicial or oversight body in the last 10 years
- A published scoring grid; the Committee's score sheet is annexed to its report

## JPC public hearing

- Hearings are public and streamed; closed session only for classified CV details
- The candidate testifies under oath and answers written JPC questions within 10 days
- Civil society may submit written questions to the JPC up to 5 days before the hearing
- Transcript and answers are published with redactions

## Binding confirmation vote

- Joint sitting of both chambers; absolute majority required; roll-call vote, results published
- The vote is **binding**: no appointment without it; a negative vote returns the process to the
  shortlist stage (see diagram)

## Appointment and term

- After confirmation, CSAT adopts the appointment decision within 15 days
- Fixed 5-year term, renewable once; appointments scheduled outside election years
- No removal during the last 6 months of a parliamentary term except on the enumerated grounds

## Removal grounds (closed list)

Removal requires a reasoned JPC finding adopted by a two-thirds majority and confirmed in plenary.
Grounds, exclusively:

1. Final criminal conviction
2. Medically certified incapacity
3. False declaration before Parliament or in vetting documents
4. Final Constitutional Court finding that the holder's acts violated the Constitution
5. Confirmed serious violation of the conflict-of-interest or cooling-off regimes (docs 15–16)

## Comparative precedents

| Precedent | Mechanism illustrated |
| --- | --- |
| Bulgaria 2025 — parliamentary appointment reform proposal | Public nomination and committee hearing before plenary votes on service heads; blocks unilateral executive removal of the SANS chairman |
| Liberia — TI Government Defence Integrity Index (2026) | Independent vetting panel with published criteria for the NSA head; de-links the service from factional politics |
| North Macedonia — post-2015 reforms | Statutory ban on active party membership for service leadership; committee hearing as condition of appointment |
| Ukraine 2024 — SBU reform | Civilian, competitively selected leadership; restriction of the service's mandate as a governance tool |
| U.S. Senate confirmation | Adversarial public hearing, sworn testimony, recorded committee and floor votes as minimum standard |
