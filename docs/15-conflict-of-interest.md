# 15 · Conflict of Interest — In-Service Rules

> DEMO // UNCLASSIFIED — SIMULATED DATA — NO REAL SYSTEMS

## Scope

Applies to all staff of SRI, SIE, STS, SPP and DNSC. Each official files an annual declaration with
the IG (`schemas/conflict-of-interest-declaration.schema.json`), verified by cross-checks and
enforced through the annual cycle in `diagrams/conflict-of-interest-cycle.mmd`.

## Prohibitions and monitoring mechanisms

| Area | Prohibition | Monitoring mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Financial holdings | No equity or debt in entities under current investigation by the declarant's service, or holding contracts with it; holdings above EUR 10,000 must sit in an approved blind trust | ANI cross-check; IG random audit; bank notification duty for transactions above EUR 10,000 |
| Immediate family | Spouse/partner, parents and children's financial interests in relevant sectors declared annually; no family member may hold a controlling stake in a service vendor | ANI family records; procurement cross-check |
| External employment | Any second employment, even uncompensated, requires written IG approval; no consultancies in the security or defence sector | Payroll cross-checks (ANAF); IG approval register |
| Academic affiliations | Teaching and research allowed if declared and within statutory compensation limits; no paid affiliation with foreign-government-funded institutions without IG approval | Declaration review; IG spot checks |
| Foreign contacts | Official contacts beyond routine duty reported within 5 working days; private contacts with foreign intelligence officers reported immediately; foreign travel reported in advance | Counterintelligence liaison logs; travel-report reconciliation |
| Political activity | No party membership, no elected office, no campaign activity, no partisan public statements while in service; family members' political roles disclosed | Electoral-authority (AEP) cross-check; media monitoring |
| Information exploitation | No use of classified or duty-acquired information for private gain; media contacts only through the press office | Media monitoring; leak investigations; IG review of publication patterns |

## Structural separation: procurement vs. operational analysis

- Procurement sits under a Deputy Director for Administration, never under an operational deputy
- Procurement panels may not include anyone who operationally evaluates vendors' products
  ("evaluator as buyer" is prohibited)
- Contracts above EUR 500,000 require ITAP technical validation and JPC notification
- The IG audits the procurement–operations separation annually as a standing item

## Consequences

- Confirmed violations trigger mandatory removal proceedings
- False declarations are referred criminally under Art. 326 of the Criminal Code
- The declaration itself carries a criminal-penalty certification clause (see schema)
